About Brandon Byars

Brandon Byars is an independent singer, songwriter and producer based in Plano, Texas. He writes the songs, sings them, produces the records and runs the marketing himself. There is no label behind him, because he is the label.

His debut album, Last Call Carolina, is 25 tracks of country storytelling with Carolina in its bones and 808s underneath: mountain roads, cheap pours, old cassettes and the long way home.

BMI songwriter · Writes, performs and owns his masters.
