## S03 recipe-v2 — EXTERIOR TRUCK (queued)

**Job:** “Ridge line fading” without going inside the cab.

**Duration:** 8–10s. Seedance 2.0 1080p.

Weathered hollow white work pickup seen entirely from **outside**, driving away down a two-lane Blue Ridge road, camera on a vehicle-to-the-side tracking shot slightly behind the rear quarter, the mountain ridge recedes with real parallax as the truck moves, empty cab visible only as dark glass with **no face, no silhouette dummy, no mannequin**, bed looks used and hollow, red clay shoulder, golden hour. Continuous track. End on the truck smaller in frame and the ridge still moving, not a freeze.

### If queued take fails

**Fallback A — even wider, no glass:** High side hill, camera pans with the truck as a small object on the road, ridge weather moving, no readable cab interior at all.

**Fallback B — road only:** Camera on the centerline looking backward as if left behind, truck recedes, ridge and sky have independent cloud motion. No rearview prop.

**Never:** interior cab, mirror, driver, parked truck with a zoom.

## S13 recipe-v2 — WIDER BANJO (queued)

**Job:** “Banjo rolling in my chest” without hero anatomy.

**Duration:** 8–10s. Seedance 2.0 1080p.

Wide or medium-wide Appalachian porch at golden hour, a five-string banjo resting against a wooden chair or played by a distant anonymous figure whose **hands are small in frame** (full body at least 20 feet from camera), camera slow dolly-in that **stops at medium-wide** and never reaches a hand CU, strings can vibrate, wood and head and fifth-string peg readable as a real banjo, wind in trees, cabin behind. End on the instrument in environment, stable, hands if visible still small.

### If queued take fails

**Fallback A — no player:** Banjo hanging on a cabin wall or lying on a quilt on the porch, camera slow orbit, a breeze moves the strings/strap, no hands in frame at all.

**Fallback B — over-shoulder extremely wide:** Player is a silhouette in a doorway, banjo body catches light, camera stays on the porch landscape, instrument is a shape not a fingering lesson.

**Never:** medium regen of hands on the neck; “fix the fingers” close-up; first-person looking down at frets.
